## 🎨 PRODUCTION NOTES FOR CREATOR

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Casual, real, slightly opinionated — like talking to a friend |
| **Pacing** | Fast cuts during problem section (3–15s), slower + confident during product demo |
| **Lighting** | Natural light preferred — no heavy studio lighting (keeps it authentic) |
| **Outfit** | Gym wear OR campus casual — match target audience vibe |
| **B-Roll Must-Haves** | Watch face close-up, notification ping, fitness stats, battery screen, wrist-on-run shot |
| **Music** | Lo-fi upbeat OR trending audio (platform-matched). Keep it low in mix — voice priority |
| **Caption Hook** | Mirror the spoken hook as the first line of the caption |
| **Hashtags (TikTok/Reels)** | `#SmartWatch #NoiseSmarwatch #FitnessTracker #AfffordableSmartwatch #CollegeLife #GadgetUnder3000` |
